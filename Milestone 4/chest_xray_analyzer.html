How it works

Upload your scan

Drag and drop any chest X-ray in PNG or JPG format. Images are processed locally on your server and never leave your machine.

Dual-model inference

EfficientNet-B0 classifies all 15 pathologies simultaneously. Faster R-CNN then localises each finding with clean bounding boxes.

Review the results

Findings are ranked by confidence with colour-coded bars. Toggle between the original and annotated view. Every scan is saved to history.

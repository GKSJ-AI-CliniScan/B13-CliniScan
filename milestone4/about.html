Transforming Radiology with AI

CliniScan is an advanced AI-powered Neural Network designed to assist radiologists in rapidly identifying 14 distinct lung abnormalities. Built upon the robust YOLOv8 architecture and utilizing the extensive VinBigData Chest X-ray dataset, it acts as a secondary diagnostic layer.

Our mission is to reduce diagnostic delays, prioritize critical patient scans in radiology queues, and improve diagnostic confidence without replacing the critical expertise of specialized medical professionals.

  • 14 Classes of Abnormality Detection
  • Real-time Inference (Under 20ms)
  • Secure & Compliant Data Handling
THE CHALLENGE

Why Healthcare Needs AI Now

Delayed Diagnosis

Manual review of hundreds of daily x-rays causes severe bottlenecks, delaying critical patient treatments when every hour matters.

Radiologist Burnout

The global shortage of trained radiologists leads to immense workloads, causing severe fatigue and reducing overall diagnostic efficiency.

Human Error

Subtle, early-stage abnormalities are easily missed by the human eye, especially during long, exhausting medical shifts.

TECH STACK

Powered by Cutting-Edge AI

Every component is purpose-built for high-throughput medical imaging with clinical-grade reliability.

Detection Model

YOLOv8x

State-of-the-art real-time object detection for precise bounding box localization of 14 lung abnormalities at radiologist-grade accuracy.

14 classes detected
Classification Model

DenseNet121

Deep residual CNN pre-trained on CheXNet achieving over 90% AUC on chest pathology classification, providing probabilistic confidence scores.

>90% AUC score
Dataset

VinBigData CXR

Trained and validated on the VinBigData Chest X-ray Grand Challenge dataset — 18,000+ annotated scans from 17 expert radiologists.

18K+ annotated scans
Backend

Flask + Python

Lightweight, production-ready REST API serving inference results in milliseconds, with DICOM/JPEG pre-processing pipelines built-in.

<20ms avg inference
Frontend

Vanilla JS + HTML5

A zero-dependency, fully responsive UI with live canvas rendering of bounding boxes, glassmorphic design, and particle animations.

100% client-side rendering
Reporting

jsPDF + html2canvas

Generates instant, patient-specific structured PDF diagnostic reports with annotated findings, confidence scores, and model metrics.

1-click PDF export
FEATURES

Built for the Modern Clinician

Every feature is designed to fit seamlessly into clinical workflows without disruption.

Dual-Model Inference

Simultaneously runs YOLOv8 for localization and DenseNet121 for classification, providing both "where" and "what" in a single scan.

Bounding Box Overlay

Color-coded, interactive bounding boxes drawn directly on the x-ray image for fast visual verification of detected regions.

PDF Report Export

Export a structured medical summary PDF instantly, including patient name, findings, confidence scores, and annotated scan.

Clinician Dashboard

Track scan history, patient records, high-risk flags, and overall diagnostic statistics from a centralized analytics dashboard.

DICOM Support

Natively processes both standard JPEG/PNG and DICOM (.dcm) formats used by hospital PACS and radiology imaging systems.

Secure & Compliant

All scans are processed securely. Designed with HIPAA-aligned data handling principles to protect sensitive patient information.
